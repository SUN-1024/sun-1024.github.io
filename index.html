Medical images,

I am a lecturer at the School of Information Engineering, Hangzhou Medical College. I received my Ph.D. in Information Science from the Japan Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (JAIST). My research brings together medical image analysis, vision–language learning, and privacy-aware artificial intelligence.

我从事医学图像分析、医学影像与文本多模态学习，以及隐私保护人工智能研究。
