I am a lecturer at the School of Information Engineering, Hangzhou Medical College. I received my Ph.D. in Information Science from the Japan Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (JAIST).

My research interests include medical image analysis, vision–language learning and radiology report generation, and privacy-aware machine learning. I work on models that connect medical images with clinical language, capture changes across images, and support collaborative learning. Feel free to contact me if you are interested in these topics.
